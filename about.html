I thought I was the good guy.
Then I saw the video.

My wife at the time filmed me during an argument. I thought I was making my point. On screen, I saw myself using anger to control her. My first instinct was to delete the video.

I could recognize a pattern and still repeat it. Changing meant practicing a different response, then following through when the pressure returned.

Sometimes that work was ordinary. When Winnie's garage door broke, we weren't living together yet. I caught myself thinking it wasn't my job. Then I found someone to repair it, negotiated the price, and saw the job through.

ARC means Aware, Release, Choose & Commit. It grew through my relationship with Winnie and the work she was already doing. It is our shared practice.

Stop Leaking Power

A personal account of anger, avoidance, responsibility, and the practice of holding your ground.

For me, commitment meant deciding in advance that anger would no longer be my authority.

Not that it would never arise.
But that it would never lead.
